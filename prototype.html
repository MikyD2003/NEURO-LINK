Network & CORS troubleshooting

“Failed to fetch” usually means the browser blocked the request before Discord could answer. Confirm the URL is a Discord webhook, then check browser extensions, VPN rules, and network policy.

Discord webhook endpoints generally allow direct browser requests. If your environment strips cross-origin requests, use this console from a permitted origin or relay through infrastructure you control. No relay is included here.
