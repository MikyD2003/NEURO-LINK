Network & CORS troubleshooting

“Failed to fetch” usually means the browser blocked the request before Discord could answer. Confirm the webhook URL or bot channel, then check browser extensions, VPN rules, and network policy.

Both transport modes make direct cross-origin requests. Bot-token mode also sends an authorization header, which can trigger a browser preflight; if your environment blocks it, a client-only relay is not possible.
